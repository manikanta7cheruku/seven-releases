# SEVEN — Private AI Voice Assistant

Your personal Jarvis. Runs 100% on your machine. No cloud. No data leaves your device.

## System Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit only)
- 8 GB RAM minimum (16 GB recommended)
- 10 GB free disk space (for AI models downloaded on first run)
- Internet connection required only for first-time setup

## What happens on first launch

1. Setup wizard opens
2. AI libraries install automatically (2-4 GB, takes 5-10 min)
3. Ollama installs automatically (180 MB)
4. You choose your AI model (2-8 GB download)
5. After setup — Seven runs 100% offline forever

## Privacy

- No microphone data ever leaves your device
- No conversation history sent anywhere  
- All AI processing is local
- Only anonymous usage statistics sent (can be disabled)
